Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The Story Parade app is used only by the owner of the Story Parade YouTube channel to upload and manage the channel's own videos, playlists and statistics through the YouTube Data API.

  • The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, with the owner's permission.
  • It does not collect, store or share personal information about viewers or any other people.
  • The sign-in token is stored privately by the owner and is never sold or shared.
  • The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.
